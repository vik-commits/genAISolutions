# EVT-001 Disruption Summary

## What Happened
Shipment SHP-1001 from Shenzhen Precision Motion was held at Yantian port due to congestion; carrier rolled booking to next vessel. Original ETA 2026-10-06 shifted to 2026-10-18 (12-day delay). Affected SKUs: MTR-220 (1,200 units) and CTL-500 (800 units) destined for DAL-DC.

## Impact
**Critical Severity**: Four at-risk orders totaling $120,800 revenue at risk and $9,300 penalty exposure. Two Tier A customers directly impacted:
- Apex Robotics (SO-9003): 9 days late, $36K revenue, $4.5K penalty
- Meridian Medical (SO-9005): 4 days late, $47.5K revenue, $4.8K penalty

## Recovery Plan
**Selected Options**: OPT-3 + OPT-8
- **OPT-3**: Alternate supplier SUP-MX02 (Monterrey Motores) for 510 units MTR-220, arriving 2026-10-09. Cost: $6,630. Protects both Tier A customers.
- **OPT-8**: DC transfer of 100 units CTL-500 from CHI-DC to DAL-DC, arriving 2026-10-02. Cost: $154. Protects SO-9010 (Brazos Packaging).

**Result**: Zero orders remain late after plan execution. All penalty exposure avoided.

## Cost & Approvals
**Total Incremental Cost**: $6,784 | **Penalty Avoided**: $9,300 | **Net Benefit**: $2,516
**Approval Required**: Operations Manager (critical Tier A protection scenario)

## Open Risks
1. Low confidence in 2026-10-18 ETA; daily supplier status checks mandatory
2. SUP-MX02 reliability 0.93 (7% non-delivery risk); PO acceptance confirmation required within 24 hours
3. CHI-DC inventory reduced to 120 units above safety stock post-transfer; monitor for secondary stockout
4. Dual-receipt dependency: failure of either source reverts SO-9003 and SO-9005 to late status; escalation protocol required
